What's included

Everything You Need to Study Smarter

PDF Text Extraction

Automatically extracts readable text from standard PDFs using pypdf. Scanned pages are processed using Gemini Vision OCR as a fallback.

YouTube Transcript Extraction

Paste any YouTube URL with available captions. CogniStudy fetches the full transcript and turns it into structured study notes.

AI-Powered Summarization

Gemini AI processes your content and generates well-structured notes with headings, key concepts, definitions and takeaways.

AI Quiz Generation

Each upload automatically generates a bank of multiple-choice and short-answer questions directly from your study material.

Performance Tracking

Every quiz attempt is logged with a score and timestamp. View your attempt history and see whether you're improving over time.

Secure & Private

Every material, summary, and quiz is scoped to your account. Other users can never access or view your data.
